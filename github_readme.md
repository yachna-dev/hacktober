# 🎧 Atmosphere & Nostalgia Jukebox

> **Bring home the authentic sounds, vibes, and nostalgia of everyday Indian moments—no matter where you are.**

---

## ✨ Overview

Have you ever missed the chaotic energy of a local bus ride, the cozy quiet of a power cut on a rainy evening, or the intense rush of *Diwali ki safai* with family? 

**Atmosphere & Nostalgia Jukebox** is an interactive web experience designed to transport you straight into quintessential Indian scenarios. By pairing curated music playlists with custom ambient background sounds—such as the conductor calling out stops, the bubbling of chai, or the hum of a ceiling fan dying out during a power outage—we deliver the exact *vibe* and nostalgia of everyday moments.

---

## 🎭 Featured Themes & Scenarios

Explore a wide collection of immersive ambient music scenarios:

* 🚌 **Bus Wale Bhaiya:** Relive the local bus journey complete with conductor calls, whistle sounds, and upbeat travel tracks.
* ☕ **Chai Ki Tapri:** Chill with ambient street murmurs, boiling tea sounds, and relaxing acoustic/lo-fi tunes.
* 🎺 **Baraat:** Feel the energy of a street wedding with loud dhols, cheering crowds, and high-energy dance anthems.
* 🧽 **Bartan Dhona:** Make household chores fun with rhythmic background soundscapes and catchy upbeat tracks.
* 🌧️ **Barish:** Experience cozy rainy days with gentle rainfall sounds, thunder, and soothing melodies.
* 🧹 **Diwali Ki Safai:** Get motivated for spring cleaning with energetic Bollywood classics and ambient home sounds.
* 🎶 **Qawwali:** Transport yourself to a soulful mehfil with applause, classic rhythm beats, and timeless qawwalis.
* 💈 **Nai Wale Bhaiya:** Sit back in the barber shop chair with scissor-snapping ambient audio and relaxing radio tunes.
* 💡 **Light Chali Gayi:** Nostalgic power-cut vibes complete with inverter hums, night crickets, and acoustic acoustic tracks.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Immersive Audio Mix:** Carefully crafted audio tracks blending music with authentic situational background SFX.
* **Instant Nostalgia:** Designed for anyone looking to reconnect with the comfort and chaos of Indian culture.
* **Theme-Based UI:** Intuitive interface reflecting the mood and color palettes of each scenario.
* **Seamless Audio Control:** Play, pause, adjust volume, and switch scenarios effortless with a clean music player interface.

## 🚀 Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (or specify React / Vue / Tailwind CSS if applicable)
